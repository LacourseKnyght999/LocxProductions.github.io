Trust & support

What to check before you install, what happens to your data, and where to get help.

Downloads and verification

Every staged release ships a checksum manifest beside the installer. Compare the SHA-256 hash of your download with the published value before running it. Storyboard Studio and 3D Studio include hashes in their release folders; FreeChat ships SHA256.txt with its builds.

Privacy and data

Our tools are local by default. Storyboard Studio runs on localhost, 3D Studio talks to local services, and LOCX Legal keeps its authority index on the machine. Optional image providers, hosted model APIs and remote queries are clearly labelled and use keys you supply. We do not claim that every feature is offline in every configuration.

Known limits

Some builds are unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first launch. Legal research is not legal representation. 3D Studio requires a compatible GPU for generation workflows and uses separately installed model packs. AisleSmith builds visual fixtures, not certified racking. Check each product page for its specific prerequisites.

Updates and refunds

Free products receive updates through their release channel. Paid products will list licence terms, update scope and refund conditions on the product page before purchase. We do not take payment for a product that is still in a pending release state.

Getting help

Email hello@locxindustries.com with the product name, version, operating system and what you expected to happen. For installation problems, include the hash of the file you downloaded and any error text.

Privacy

Our tools run locally by default. Optional remote providers are labelled in each product, use keys you supply, and are never enabled silently. We do not collect project content, keys or personal files through the website. The website uses privacy-conscious, cookieless analytics.

Licensing

FreeChat and AisleSmith are MIT and GPL-3.0-or-later respectively, with their notices included in the distributed packages. For paid products, the product page will state the exact licence and what is included before purchase. We do not restrict lawful redistribution of open-source components.

Commercial terms

We do not take payment for a product that is still pending release gates. When a product is available, its page will state price, currency, included files, supported versions, update scope and refund terms before checkout. No subscription is required to use software advertised as local and independently usable.

Installation troubleshooting

Keep portable builds in a writable folder. If an engine reports as missing, run the setup checker shipped with the product and point it at the correct local path. If Windows blocks an unsigned installer, verify the hash first, then choose the documented "more info" path. Do not disable your antivirus.
