A Blender add-on for industrial scenes.

AisleSmith is a Blender add-on for building shelves, racks and benches from parameters. The pictures are renders from the add-on itself.

BUILD 0.1.0 · BLENDER ADD-ON

AisleSmith: Industrial Scene Builder for Blender

For environment artists building warehouses, workshops and retail sets.

Nine modular fixture generators in one panel: pallet racks, boltless shelving, cantilevers, retail gondolas, workbenches, pallets, crates, totes and safety rails. Generate one fixture, a row or a grid, then adjust spacing, bays, shelf levels, palettes and a repeatable variation seed.

  • Nine parametric fixture families, pure Python and editable meshes
  • Single, row or grid placement with seeded cargo variation
  • Five material palettes and three detail levels
  • Runs locally in Blender, with no account, cloud service or texture downloads

Works on Blender 4.2.2 LTS and 5.3.0 on Windows. GPL-3.0-or-later, with the full Python source included.
