Vibe Coded.
Precision Built.

LOCX Industries is an independent software studio founded by LK999 & AB696, built on a simple conviction: artificial intelligence should raise the bar for software, not lower it.

In a market flooded with rushed, disposable AI tools, we build the opposite. We embrace the creative momentum of vibe coding, then back it with deliberate architecture, thoughtful design, and an uncompromising standard of quality. Every product we release is crafted to solve real-world bottlenecks, push past conventional limits, and put tools in your hands that feel genuinely ahead of their time.

From next-generation developer tools and local AI infrastructure to creative software and experimental technologies, LOCX Industries builds instruments for makers, engineers, and visionaries who refuse to settle for ordinary.

We don't ship software just because AI makes it fast to generate. We build it because we see a smarter, bolder way to work—and we want you to experience it.

Human imagination. Machine velocity. Zero compromise.
