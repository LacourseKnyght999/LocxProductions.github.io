04 / About

Vibe Coded.
Precision Built.

LOCX Industries is an independent software studio. LK999 and AB696 started it because AI makes it cheap to produce software, and we wanted to see what happens when that speed is paired with the boring, careful part.

A lot of AI-made software feels disposable. We're trying to do the opposite. We prototype fast, then spend the unglamorous time on the parts that matter: architecture that holds up, interfaces that make sense, and builds that work on a clean machine.

The things here solve problems we've hit ourselves. Keeping a storyboard in one place. Getting a usable 3D asset out of one reference image. Searching real federal law without sending someone's matter to a server.

We're not interested in shipping something because a model can produce it in an afternoon. If it's on this site, it's because we'd be annoyed without it.
