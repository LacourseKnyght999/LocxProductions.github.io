LOCX 3D Studio

For 3D artists who need assets made locally, not in someone else's account.

A local, offline 3D asset workshop. Image-to-geometry, texturing, mesh optimization, rigging helpers, QA and GLB export in one Electron app. Nothing is uploaded — it talks only to local loopback services.

  • 12/12 automated ship-gate checks passing
  • Lightweight hostable payload — no weights bundled

  • Portable paths: installs and moves without hardcoded drives

LOCX Legal

A local research workspace for organizing matters and authorities — not a substitute for counsel.

A development alpha staged for internal review: a matter workspace, a real index of 49,092 federal authority sections, source-backed research answers, and explicit local/remote model controls. The sample matter and AI output in these screenshots are fictional and come from a local mock provider.

  • Matter records with evidence, timeline, claims and tasks
  • Real federal statute search across 49,092 indexed sections
  • Source-backed memorandum answers with visible citations
  • Local-first AI configuration; remote queries blocked without consent

Not approved for sale or promotion. Legal research is not legal representation. Authorities, jurisdiction and deadlines must be verified by qualified counsel.
