LOCX Storyboard Studio

For directors, writers and worldbuilders who think in shots.

A standalone local web app for turning scenes into shots. Multi-project storyboards, camera and prompt notes, a reference library, and a freeform concept mixboard. Image providers are optional and only run when you configure one.

  • Scene & shot editor with reordering and JSON export
  • Character, location, prop and costume references
  • Concept Mixboard with undo, zoom and PNG export
  • Optional image generation and an MCP agent adapter

LOCX 3D Studio

For 3D artists who need assets made locally, not in someone else's account.

A local, offline 3D asset workshop. Image-to-geometry, texturing, mesh optimization, rigging helpers, QA and GLB export in one Electron app. Nothing is uploaded. It talks only to local loopback services.

  • 12/12 automated checks passing
  • Lightweight payload of about 379 MB, with no model weights bundled
  • Setup CLI discovers and wires local model packs
  • Portable paths: installs and moves without hardcoded drives

LOCX Legal

A local research workspace for matters and authorities. It is not a substitute for a lawyer.

A development alpha staged for internal review: a matter workspace, a real index of 49,092 federal authority sections, source-backed research answers, and explicit local/remote model controls. The sample matter and AI output in these screenshots are fictional and come from a local mock provider.

  • Matter records with evidence, timeline, claims and tasks
  • Real federal statute search across 49,092 indexed sections
  • Source-backed memorandum answers with visible citations
  • Local-first AI configuration; remote queries blocked without consent

Legal research is not legal representation. Verify authorities, jurisdiction and deadlines with a qualified lawyer.
