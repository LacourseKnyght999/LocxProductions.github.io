Good questions

Before you ask.

Can I buy one today?

Not yet. Our packaged builds are still pending approval, and the Blender add-on is still in verification. When that changes, the release library moves the product to "Approved for Sale" and this site updates with it. The list above is how you hear about it first.

What happens after I register?

Nothing until there's something real to say. Then you get one email per release with the build, requirements, known limits and licence terms. No countdowns, no drip campaign.

Does my work leave my machine?

The desktop tools are local by default: Storyboard Studio runs on localhost, 3D Studio talks only to local loopback services, and LOCX Legal keeps its authority index and matter data on the machine. External image generation and remote model queries are opt-in and clearly labelled.

Why isn't there a price yet?

Because pricing comes after licence clearance and clean-machine verification, not before. We'd rather give you a real number once than a placeholder now.

Can I talk to a human instead?

Yes. Use the contact address below and tell us what you're trying to make. We read every message and answer the ones we can actually help with.
