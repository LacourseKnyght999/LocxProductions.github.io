Tripp Attack

A five-card directional battler on a 3×3 board, built around clean reads and competitive multiplayer.

Game / Strategy / MultiplayerProject showcase
The project

Simple board. Mean decisions.

The core idea is deliberately tight: place a card, compare the touching sides, take control when your number wins. The work is in making every capture obvious, every turn quick, and every hand feel good to finish.
