A note from the studio

Two creators.
One workbench.

Most of our projects start the same way: we're making something, hit a frustrating problem, and end up building a tool for it.

We needed a storyboard that stays on one machine, a 3D workshop that keeps our references local, and a way to research federal law locally. So we built them. LOCX is where we share the tools we think other people might find useful, too.

We'd rather show you what the software does than promise what it might do someday.

— LK & AB
